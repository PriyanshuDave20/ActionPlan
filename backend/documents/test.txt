nvidia/Nemotron-3-Embed-1B-BF16 correct?

Yes. nvidia/Nemotron-3-Embed-1B-BF16 is the correct Hugging Face model ID.

It's NVIDIA's ~1.14B parameter text-embedding model, designed specifically for retrieval, semantic search, and RAG. It produces 2048-dimensional embeddings and supports 34 languages.

For your RAG project, you'd load it like:

from sentence_transformers import SentenceTransformer

model = SentenceTransformer("nvidia/Nemotron-3-Embed-1B-BF16")

One important detail: NVIDIA recommends using:

query: <user question>
passage: <document chunk>

for retrieval. The model's embeddings are already L2-normalized, so cosine similarity and dot product are equivalent.

Also, BF16 matters: the checkpoint is about 2.28 GB, so it's much more substantial than something like all-MiniLM-L6-v2.
